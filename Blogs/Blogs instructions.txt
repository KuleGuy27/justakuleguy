- Make a new folder with the ISO 8601 date + topic of your blog
- make a blog.html file
- make a folder in Abyss.to and Internet Archive and upload your videos there (if necessary)
- copy or create any images into the blog folder as you see fit

 - in the blog list (index), copy and paste the article tag and edit the: hrefs, h2 (title), image, date, description, tags, and blogger link

- in the blog posts itself; make the site title to "[title] | kuleguy", add h1 title, add dates, main image thumbnail, yt vid index (if have), change table of contents, add headings and text
