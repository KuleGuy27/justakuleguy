Clapping my hands for World Clap Day (August 16, 2026)

16 August 2026

Filmed on my Sony CX240 handycam mounted on a tripod and saved using an HDMI capture card.

World Clap day is an event hosted by a London-based Italian guy named Andrea Bechis where everyone basically claps their hands at the exact same time for 1 second on:
August 16, 2026 at 10:00 AM NYC TIME

I got a notification on my calendar 10 minutes before the time and I rushed quickly to make this video. Now because I live in the great state of Texas, I had a chance to clap my hands for this just before I went to church with my family. Nothing really of note happened, I'm not sure if there will be another thing like this next year or ever again...
